Codenames Quick Rules

The spymasters of each team know the codenames of all their secret agents. Their teammates do not know which agent is hiding behind each name. Both teams race to make contact with all of their agents first.

On their turn, a spymaster gives a one-word clue that connects several cards on the board. Operatives try to guess their team's words while avoiding the cards that belong to the opposing team.

And everyone wants to avoid the assassin.
